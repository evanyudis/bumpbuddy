## Design Context

### Users
Expecting mothers (both first-time and experienced) who need a high-efficiency tool to track their pregnancy journey. The app is used in varied contexts: from daily movement tracking to high-stress labor scenarios, requiring both speed and clarity.

### Brand Personality
**Voice & Tone**: Supportive, rest-assured, and reliable.
**Personality**: Like a calm, expert companion who is also warm and approachable.
**Emotional Goals**: Calm and in control.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Visual Tone**: Dynamic and Playful. While the purpose is serious, the interface should feel "alive" through micro-interactions and tactile feedback.
**Theme**: Equal weight on Light and Dark modes, leveraging the "iOS 26 Liquid Glass" aesthetic for a premium, futuristic feel.
**Anti-references**: Clinical, dry, or overly complex medical UIs.

### Design Principles
1. **Calm Clarity**: Use generous spacing and clear typography to reduce cognitive load, especially during critical moments like labor.
2. **Tactile Playfulness**: Every interaction should provide satisfying visual or motion feedback, making the "job" of tracking feel delightful.
3. **High-Efficiency Interaction**: Prioritize one-tap actions for core logging tasks. Minimize friction to respect the user's focus and time.
4. **Reliable Stability**: Despite the playful visuals, the system must feel solid and trustworthy. Calculations and data persistence should be transparent and rock-solid.
